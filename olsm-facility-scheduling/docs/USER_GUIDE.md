to do. Find your role in the contents and start there; the reference sections at
the end apply to everyone.

## 1. Getting an account

**There is no sign-up page, and that is deliberate.** Every account is created by
the athletic office. Signing in with a Microsoft or Google account links to an
account that already exists — it never creates one. Somebody who is not on the
People list cannot get in.

### How you get one

The athletic office adds you, and you receive **one link** to choose a password.
That link:

- works **once**
- expires after **seven days**
- stops working the moment a newer one is sent to you

Open it, choose a password (at least 12 characters, with upper case, lower case
and a number), and you are signed in. That is the whole setup.

### If your link expired

Ask the athletic office to send another. They can do this from **Admin →
People**. There is no "forgot password" link on the sign-in page — for a system
with about fifty known users, asking a person is both simpler and safer than an
automated one, and it leaves a record.

Go to **/sign-in**. Depending on how the school has configured things you may
see up to three options:

| Option | Who it is for |
|---|---|
| Continue with your OLSM Microsoft account | Staff, once Microsoft sign-in is switched on |
| Continue with your OLSM Google account | Staff, if Google Workspace sign-in is used instead |
| Email address and password | Everyone else, and a fallback for staff |

Where you land depends on your role:

- **Coaches and staff** → the Calendar
- **Outside groups** → My requests
- **Facilities** → the Setup board
- **Business office** → Reports

### Signing out

The **Sign out** button is in the top right on every page. Use it on a shared
computer. Sessions last 14 days otherwise.

## 3. What your booking needs

This is the table that explains almost every question people ask. What a booking
requires depends on **what the activity is**, not on who you are. A head coach
running paid Saturday lessons clears the same gates as an outside company,
because the activity is the same.

| What you are booking | Approval | Contract | Waiver | Insurance | Payment |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Contest / game / meet | None — confirmed at once | — | — | — | — |
| In-season team practice *(head coach)* | None — confirmed at once | — | — | — | — |
| In-season team practice *(assistant coach)* | Your head coach | — | — | — | — |
| Out-of-season / open gym *(head coach)* | None — confirmed at once | — | — | — | — |
| Non-athletic school event | Athletic office | — | — | — | — |
| School camp or clinic | Athletic office | Yes | Yes | — | Yes |
| Club / travel / AAU team | Athletic office | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Private lessons / personal training | Athletic office | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Third-party rental | Athletic office | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Maintenance / blackout *(admin only)* | None | — | — | — | — |

**Nothing is confirmed until every column that applies is complete.** Until then
your slot is *held* — nobody else can take it, but it is not yours yet.

The athletic office can change any of this at **Admin → Approval rules**. A
booking freezes the rules that applied when it was created, so changing a rule
never disturbs bookings already in progress.

## 4. For coaches

### Your annual agreement comes first

Before you can book anything you need a current **Annual Coach Agreement**. It
covers facility rules, supervision, emergency and AED procedure, concussion
protocol, and an undertaking not to run paid instruction on school facilities
without booking it as such.

Go to **My documents**. If yours is missing or expired you will see a button to
start one; sign it and you are clear for the rest of the school year. Agreements
expire on **31 July**, so everyone signs a fresh one each year.

If you try to book without one, the system tells you before you fill anything in
and links you straight to the page.

### Booking a practice — the 30-second path

**Book a space** in the top navigation.

1. Choose the activity type, the space, and a title
2. Set the date, start and end
3. Add headcount and any setup notes
4. **Request booking**

If you are a **head coach booking in-season practice**, it is confirmed on the
spot. No approval, no paperwork, no payment.

If you are an **assistant coach**, it goes to your head coach — who sees it under
**Approvals** and can clear it in one click.

### If the time is taken

You will be told who has it and offered **alternatives** — the same space at a
nearby hour, the same time on nearby days, and comparable spaces. Pick one and
the form fills itself in. Nothing you typed is lost.

### Your team's season — standing practice time

**My team** is where recurring practice lives. Instead of booking eighteen
Tuesdays one at a time, ask for the block once:

1. Choose the season, your sport, team level and space
2. Tick the days of the week
3. Set the start and end time
4. Optionally narrow the date range — useful when your competition calendar is
   shorter than the season that contains it
5. Add a note making your case, and submit

Nothing is reserved yet. The athletic office reviews it, and you can see the
status on the same page. You can **withdraw** a request nobody has decided.

If it is **denied**, you see the reason in the reviewer's own words. If it is
**approved**, the sessions appear on the calendar.

You can also **release** a standing block day you do not need — the time goes
straight back to open inventory and anyone waiting on that window is told.

### Approving your assistants' requests

**Approvals** in the top navigation shows requests where you are the named
approver — your sport only. Approve or deny with a note.

### Holding too much

A non-admin account can hold **five** slots at once while paperwork is
outstanding. Confirmed bookings do not count. If you hit the limit, finish or
cancel one, or ask the athletic office.

## 5. For the athletic office

### Adding people

**Admin → People** is where every account begins.

1. **Add a person** — name, email, role, optional phone, optional organisation
2. They receive one link to choose a password

For staff, use their OLSM email address so Microsoft sign-in matches it. For an
outside group, fill in the organisation name — an existing organisation of the
same name is reused, so a club's insurance and invoices stay on one record.

The page shows who has been added but never signed in, and lets you **resend a
sign-in link**. That button is also the password reset.

**You cannot create an account with a role above your own.** The Athletic
Director can appoint another director; the athletic office cannot.

> **Set up a second Athletic Director.** With only one, a single forgotten
> password locks the whole system, because the reset button lives behind the
> sign-in you cannot get past.

### Deciding requests

**Approvals** shows everything waiting, with conflicts flagged inline, and the
standing-block requests coaches have filed. For a standing block you see how
many sessions it claims and what it collides with before you decide.

A denial requires a reason. The requester sees it verbatim, so write it for them.

### Reviewing certificates of insurance

Uploading a certificate is **not** the same as accepting one. A certificate
arrives as *under review*; someone has to open it and decide.

- **Accept** — the booking's insurance gate clears
- **Reject** — give a reason the renter can act on; they can upload a replacement

Both versions are kept. A rejected certificate and the reason it was rejected are
part of the record.

### Building a season

**Admin → Season allocation** is for laying down standing blocks directly and for
publishing a whole season at once. The preview shows every collision; publishing
is refused until they are resolved. Publishing turns each occurrence into a real
booking through the same path a hand-made booking uses.

### Rates, rules and blackouts

- **Admin → Rate cards** — hourly and day rates by tier
- **Admin → Approval rules** — the matrix in section 3, editable per activity
  type, including refund windows and how long a hold lasts
- **Admin → Blackouts** — maintenance and unavailable windows
- **Admin → Facilities & spaces** — names, capacity, buffer minutes, which
  spaces conflict with which

### Cancelling for the school's own reasons

When the school is the reason a booking is not happening — a rained-out field, a
closure, a schedule change — tick **refund in full regardless of the
cancellation window** when you cancel. The group is made whole and the waiver is
recorded in the audit log.

### The audit log

**Admin → Audit log** records every change, append-only. Nobody can edit or
delete an entry, including administrators. Exportable as CSV.

## 6. For outside groups

### Getting set up

Contact the athletic office. They create your account and send you a link to
choose a password. You cannot sign yourself up.

### Making a request

**My requests → Request a facility**, then four steps: when, where, what, who.

Your slot is **held from the moment you submit**, so nobody can take it while
your paperwork is in progress.

### What happens next

1. **Submitted** — the slot is checked for conflicts and held
2. **Under review** — the athletic office approves or declines
3. **Agreement and waiver** — you sign a facility use agreement and a liability
   waiver, online
4. **Insurance** — you upload a certificate of general liability insurance naming
   Orchard Lake St. Mary's Preparatory as an additional insured, valid through
   the date of your booking
5. **Payment** — you pay the invoice
6. **Confirmed** — only once everything above is complete

Your booking page shows exactly which step you are on and what it is waiting for.

### Holds expire

If the outstanding items are not finished in time, the hold releases and the time
returns to open inventory. The page tells you the deadline. Currently **72 hours**
for documents and **120 hours** for payment, unless the athletic office has
changed it.

### Cancelling

From your booking page. The refund depends on how far out you are — the schedule
is shown on the page before you confirm.

## 7. For facilities and custodial staff

**Setup board** is your page. It shows what is booked today, grouped by building,
with:

- turnover times between bookings
- setup notes from the requester
- equipment notes
- headcount

You can also **mark a space unavailable** when something breaks, which keeps it
out of the booking system until it is fixed.

A daily digest of the setup board is emailed each morning once email is
configured.

---

## 8. For the business office

**Reports** covers utilisation, revenue and compliance:

- pending approvals, awaiting documents, awaiting payment
- unpaid invoice totals
- certificates of insurance expiring within 30 days
- coaching accounts without a current agreement — these accounts cannot book
- denials, bumps and no-shows over the last 30 days

Invoices are visible per booking. Refunds are issued from the invoice page.

## 9. Reference

### Reading the calendar

**Colour is the sport.** Each sport has its own colour, and the key beside the
grid — below it on a phone — names the ones booked in the view you are looking
at. It lists only what is on screen, so it stays short.

Two things worth knowing about it:

- **Bookings with no sport are grey.** Rentals, school events and maintenance
  share one slate colour, because they are a category rather than a team.
- **Colour is a shortcut, never the whole story.** Every block also carries its
  own title, so you never have to rely on telling two colours apart. Two sports
  share a colour only when their seasons do not overlap.

**Lighter, ringed blocks are held, not confirmed.** They also say *held* in the
block. Somebody has the slot reserved while their paperwork is outstanding; if
it is not finished in time the hold releases.

The **Day** view puts spaces side by side, which is the one to use when you are
looking for a free court. **Week** puts days side by side. **Month** and
**Agenda** are for scanning further out.

### The eight facilities

| Facility | Notes |
|---|---|
| Milewski Field | Outdoor; splits into north and south halves |
| Dan Petry / Ziemba Stadium | Outdoor; field, track apron, press box, concessions |
| Dombrowski Fieldhouse | Indoor; full floor splits into Court A and Court B, plus batting cages and turf |
| Rakoczy Gymnasium | Indoor; full court splits into Court 1 and Court 2, plus auxiliary |
| St. Mary's Athletic Complex | Outdoor |
| Crew House | Boat bay |
| Weight Training Room | Indoor |
| Running Track | Outdoor |

**Spaces that overlap know about each other.** Booking "Full floor" blocks Court
A and Court B; booking Court A blocks the full floor. You cannot double-book by
choosing a different name for the same square footage.

### What a booking's status means

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pending approval | Waiting on a person to decide |
| Approved | Cleared, but still owes documents or payment |
| Awaiting documents | Agreement, waiver or insurance outstanding |
| Awaiting payment | Invoice unpaid |
| Confirmed | Everything complete; the time is yours |
| Checked in | Marked as under way on the day |
| Completed | Finished |
| Cancelled / Denied / Expired | Not happening — expired means a hold ran out |
| No-show | Booked and not used; visible in reports |

### Money

- Rentals are **$50.00 per hour**, flat, every facility, every paying requester
- Internal school use is **$0**
- Full day caps at six hours of the hourly rate

Surcharges for lights, custodial, supervision and athletic trainer coverage are
**placeholders pending the school's decision** — see `DECISIONS.md`. There is no
security deposit.

### Cancellation and refunds

Seeded defaults, editable per activity type:

- **Full refund** 14 or more days before the booking
- **50%** from 3 to 13 days
- **No refund** inside 3 days

A booking bumped by higher-priority school activity is **always refunded in
full**, regardless of the window.

### Priority

When two requests want the same space, this is the order:

> Contest → varsity practice → JV / freshman practice → school event →
> out-of-season → school camp → club / travel → private instruction →
> external rental

Higher priority can displace lower, but **never automatically** — an
administrator has to confirm it, and the displaced party is refunded in full and
told.

### Calendar subscriptions

Every facility publishes a read-only calendar feed at:

```
https://olsm-facilities.onrender.com/api/feeds/<facility-slug>.ics
```

For example `/api/feeds/rakoczy-gymnasium.ics`. Subscribe in Google Calendar,
Outlook or your phone and the schedule appears alongside your own. Confirmed
bookings only, no contact details, and it updates on its own.

## 10. When something goes wrong

**"That email address and password do not match."**
The address may not have an account, or the password is wrong. The message is
deliberately the same either way. Ask the athletic office for a sign-in link.

**"This link cannot be used."**
A sign-in link works once and expires in seven days. If you set a password a
moment ago, just sign in. Otherwise ask for a new link.

**"Your Annual Coach Agreement is not on file."**
Go to **My documents** and sign one. Expired counts as not on file.

**"You already have 5 requests holding a slot."**
Finish or cancel one, or ask the athletic office — administrators are exempt.

**The time I want is taken.**
The conflict message names what is in the way and offers alternatives. If none
work, ask the athletic office; they can see the whole calendar and may be able to
move something.

**My booking says "held, not confirmed."**
Something is still outstanding. The progress panel on the booking page names it.

**A page says I am not allowed.**
Your role does not include that screen. The page says so plainly rather than
showing an error. If you need access, the athletic office can change your role.

**Nothing is arriving by email.**
Email may not be configured yet. Until it is, the athletic office can read
sign-in links on screen and pass them on by hand. Approval notices and invoices
will not send.

## What this system deliberately does not do

Worth knowing so you do not go looking:

- **No self-registration.** Accounts come from the athletic office only.
- **No self-service password reset.** Ask the office; they send a link.
- **No two-way calendar sync.** Feeds are read-only, on purpose — an edit made in
  Google cannot re-run the conflict check, the contract or the invoice.
- **No security deposits.** A card authorisation expires long before a booking
  made two months out, so it was never the control it appeared to be.
- **No automated weather cancellation.** A rained-out booking is cancelled the
  ordinary way, with the full-refund tick.
